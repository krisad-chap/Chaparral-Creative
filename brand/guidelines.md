## 1. Who we are

Chaparral Creative, LLC is a digital marketing agency working with local governments, nonprofits, service businesses and regional brands — mostly across Texas. Founded and led by Krisa De La Cruz.

We do SEO and web optimization, content and digital strategy, social media management, paid search, and brand and creative design.

**The line we lead with:** *More traffic, more leads, more growth — digital marketing that works.*

**What we actually sell:** clarity. Most of our clients aren't short on effort; they're short on knowing which effort pays. We audit first, then build the plan from what the data says.

## 2. Voice

We sound like a capable operator, not a marketing brochure.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Direct — the point comes first | Coy, or burying the lede under throat-clearing |
| Concrete — numbers, timeframes, named outcomes | Vague about results, hiding behind "synergy" and "solutions" |
| Plain-spoken — a city manager understands it | Jargon-heavy, acronym-soaked |
| Warm — we like our clients and it shows | Chummy, over-familiar, or gimmicky |
| Confident — we've done this before | Boastful, or dismissive of what the client already built |

**Three tests before anything ships:**

1. **Would a busy client read past the first line?** If the opening sentence is scene-setting, cut it.
2. **Could a competitor put their logo on this?** If yes, it's too generic.
3. **Is there a number in it?** Not always possible — but reach for one.

---

## 3. Tone by context

Voice stays constant. Tone shifts.

- **Website & landing pages** — Confident and outcome-led. Short paragraphs. One clear action per page.
- **Proposals** — Plain and specific. Say what's included, what it costs, and when it lands. No hedging on timelines.
- **Client reporting** — Honest first, encouraging second. Lead with what moved, name what didn't, then say what changes next month.
- **Social** — Lighter, more human, still useful. Teach something small rather than announce something.
- **Bad news (a campaign underperforming, a deadline slipping)** — Straight, early, and with the fix attached. Never soften it into ambiguity.

## 4. Writing rules

**Do**

- Write in second person. "Your site," not "the client's site."
- Lead with the result, follow with the method. *"Call volume dropped a third — here's what changed."*
- Use specific nouns. "Google Business Profile," not "your local listings presence."
- Keep sentences under 25 words and paragraphs under four lines.
- Use active voice. "We rebuilt the site," not "the site was rebuilt."

**Don't**

- Don't use "leverage," "utilize," "best-in-class," "cutting-edge," "game-changing," "unlock," or "supercharge."
- Don't promise rankings or guarantee positions. Talk about traffic, leads and revenue instead.
- Don't use exclamation marks in client-facing writing. One per social post, maximum.
- Don't stack more than two adjectives on anything.
- Don't say "we're excited to announce." Just announce it.

**Naming**

- Full legal name **Chaparral Creative, LLC** on contracts, invoices, footers and the first mention in formal documents.
- **Chaparral Creative** everywhere else. Never "Chaparral" alone, never "CC."

## 5. Colour

Seven colours. Full detail and contrast pairings: `design-system/foundations/colors.html`.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Chaparral Ink | `#120b47` | Headlines, footers, dark sections, high-emphasis text |
| Signal Blue | `#3498db` | Primary actions, links, active states |
| Slate | `#2c3e50` | Body copy, secondary surfaces, navigation |
| Roadrunner Sage | `#3f5d5f` | Logo mark, editorial accents, quotes |
| Mist | `#ecf0f1` | Borders, dividers, disabled surfaces |
| Bone | `#f1f1ef` | Alternating sections, subtle surfaces |
| White | `#ffffff` | Page background, card surfaces |

**Proportion.** Roughly 70% neutral (White/Bone), 20% Ink or Slate, 10% Signal Blue and Sage combined. Blue is an accent — when everything is highlighted, nothing is.

**Accessibility.** Ink and Slate pass AAA on white at any size. Signal Blue as a *background* behind white text only clears AA as a UI component or at 18px+ — never set 16px white copy on it.

## 6. Typography

Four families, one job each. Full scale: `design-system/foundations/typography.html`.

- **Poppins** — Titles and display only. Never below 24px.
- **Montserrat** — Section headings, eyebrows, buttons, UI labels.
- **Proxima Nova** — Sub-headings within long-form content. Licensed; not on Google Fonts. **Montserrat is the approved web fallback**, so browser previews will show Subheading and Subtitle as closely related.
- **Merriweather Sans** — All running copy, captions, form text.

Don't introduce a fifth family. If something needs to feel different, change size, weight or colour — not typeface.

## 7. Logo

Full rules: `design-system/foundations/logo.html`.

A line-drawn roadrunner paired with a serif wordmark reading *Chaparral Creative, LLC*. The mark is drawn in Roadrunner Sage.

- **Clear space** — one quarter of the mark's height on all four sides. Nothing enters it.
- **Minimum size** — icon 24px on screen / 0.5in in print; horizontal lockup 160px / 1.25in.
- **Approved backgrounds** — Sage on White, Sage on Bone, White on Ink. Over photography, place the reversed mark on a solid colour block.
- **Never** — recolour outside Sage/white/Ink, stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, or rebuild the wordmark in another typeface.

Master artwork lives in the Canva brand kit. See `brand/assets/README.md`.

---

## 8. Photography & imagery

- Real work and real places over stock. Texas light, wide skies, actual main streets and actual staff.
- If stock is unavoidable, choose images with room for text and avoid anything that reads as a stock photo — no handshakes over conference tables, no laughing-at-salad.
- Apply a solid Ink overlay at 70%+ under any text sitting on an image.
- Illustration, when used, is line-based and single-weight, echoing the roadrunner mark.
