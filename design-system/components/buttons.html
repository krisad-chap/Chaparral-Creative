Buttons

Five variants, three sizes and a full state table. One primary action per view.

Variants

Five variants, one job each. Exactly one primary button per view — if two things look equally important, neither is.

Sizes

Default is 44px — the minimum comfortable touch target. Large is for hero calls to action; small for table rows and toolbars.

Rules

DoWrite labels as the action the visitor takes: "Book a consultation", not "Submit" or "Click here".
Don'tStack two primary buttons side by side. Pair primary with secondary or ghost.
DoKeep labels under four words so buttons stay on one line at mobile widths.
Don'tRemove the focus ring. Keyboard users rely on it and it's a WCAG failure.
