Type families

Four families are defined in the Canva brand kit, each bound to a specific role. Don't introduce a fifth. Proxima Nova is a licensed font with no Google Fonts equivalent — on the web it falls back to Montserrat, which is why Subheading and Subtitle look closely related in browser previews.

Title
Poppins

Geometric sans. Page titles and display headlines only. --cc-font-title

Subtitle
Montserrat

Section headings, eyebrows, UI labels, buttons. --cc-font-subtitle

Subheading
Proxima Nova

Sub-headings inside long content. Falls back to Montserrat on web. --cc-font-subheading

Body
Merriweather Sans

All running copy, captions, form text. --cc-font-body

Case study

Composition

A worked example showing the intended hierarchy end to end.

Local government

Sealy grew web traffic 340% in eight months

A single content engine replaced four disconnected channels — and gave staff one place to publish.

The problem

Residents couldn't find basic service information, and staff were re-answering the same questions by phone every week. Search visibility for the city's own name was being lost to third-party aggregators.

What we changed

We rebuilt the information architecture around resident tasks rather than departments, then layered a monthly content programme on top of it.

Rules

DoKeep line length between 45 and 75 characters for body copy. Use max-width: 68ch as the default.
Don'tSet Poppins below 24px. It loses legibility fast at body sizes — that's what Merriweather Sans is for.
DoSkip at most one level in the hierarchy. Title → Subheading is fine; Title → Caption is not.
Don'tUse all-caps anywhere except the Eyebrow style, and never for more than four words.
