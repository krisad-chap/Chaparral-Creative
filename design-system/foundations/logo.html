Logo & usage

The roadrunner mark and wordmark as vector lockups, with colourways, scale floors, clear space and misuse rules.

The mark

A line-drawn roadrunner paired with a serif wordmark reading Chaparral Creative, LLC. All three lockups are vector and use fill="currentColor", so they inherit whatever colour is set on the parent. No colour is baked into the files.

Colourways

Three approved treatments. Because the artwork uses currentColor, switching colourway is one CSS property — never edit the file itself.

Clear space

Clear space on all four sides equals one quarter of the mark's height. Nothing — type, image edge, or another logo — enters the dashed zone.

Rules

DoUse the horizontal lockup wherever there is room. It is the primary form of the identity.
Don'tRecolour outside Sage, white, or solid Ink. No gradients, no photographic fills, no drop shadows.
DoSet colour on the parent element and let currentColor do the work.
Don'tStretch, rotate, outline, or rebuild the wordmark in a different typeface.
DoPlace the reversed mark on a solid colour block when it must sit over photography.
Don'tSeparate the roadrunner from the wordmark and re-space them into a custom lockup.
